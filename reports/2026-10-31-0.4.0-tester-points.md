## Gate 1 — the Zápis tab lists the reports on this device (point 2)

### What changed for a referee

- **The Zápis tab opens on a list of the reports on the phone**, where it said *Žádný
  zápis* after every restart. First what is still being worked on, under
  *Rozpracované* (*Rozepsáno*, *Probíhá*, *Dohráno*), then what all three have signed,
  under *Potvrzené*; in each, the latest kickoff first. A row is a fixture's row in
  Zápasy without the pitch: kickoff time over the date, home over away, the status in
  the same badge as Zápasy, and the group (*6K*). The title bar says *Zápisy*.
- **Tapping a row opens the report where tapping its fixture in Zápasy would**: the
  header for *Rozepsáno*, the console for *Probíhá*, the recap for *Dohráno* and
  *Potvrzeno*.
- **Zpět on a report's first step comes back to the list**, and back from the list goes
  to Zápasy. That holds for a report opened from Zápasy too: one more step back to
  Zápasy than in 0.3.1 (judgement call 1).
- **The list follows the database**: a report started from Zápasy, or moved on in the
  wizard, is in it the next time the referee looks, without leaving the tab.
- **With no report on the phone** it says what it said: *Žádný zápis*, the same
  sentence, and *Vybrat zápas*, which goes to Zápasy.
- **Unchanged:** the tab's badge (only a match in progress); re-selecting the tab does
  not throw the referee out of the step they are on; one report open in the tab at a
  time; after a kill, a match in progress is put back so that the badge lands on the
  console, now with the list beneath it.

### Why it said *Žádný zápis*

The navigator does not survive the process, by design: the report is in the database,
and recovery is tapping its row. The tab's root was "no report open", replaced by the
first report opened, and on launch the shell put back only a match whose whistle had
gone. A report set up and not kicked off, or played and not signed, was then reachable
only from Zápasy, while the tab said there was none: what the tester saw on the iPhone.

### What was done

- **`shared`, `usecase/ObserveReports.kt`** (new): `ReportRow` (the match, its status,
  the fixture, the group, the two teams, and `resumePoint`, which is
  `MatchStatus.resumePoint()`, the one Zápasy uses) and `ReportListing` (`working`,
  `confirmed`). `ObserveReports` is a stream from `MatchRepository.observeSummaries()`:
  the summaries as a set, `distinctUntilChanged`, so a goal or a header edit is neither
  a new list nor another join to the league; each joined to `LeagueRepository.fixture`,
  and **a report whose fixture is not in league data skipped**, as
  `ObserveReportInProgress` skips it; split into unsigned and signed, each sorted by
  date and time descending, then group, pitch, fixture and match id so two reports
  never swap places.
- **`AppNavigator`**: `Destination.NoReport` is `Destination.Reports`, the tab's root
  and **beneath every report** — `openReport` and `adoptReport` build the list, then the
  report's steps up to the one opened. `openReport(matchId, resumePoint)` is the one
  call both lists open a report through (the mapping was a private function in
  `App.kt`). `adoptReport` acts **once per launch** (judgement call 2); the stack check
  it had is gone, redundant with that.
- **`ui/reports/`** (new): `ReportsViewModel` (`stateIn`, `WhileSubscribed(5 s)` as the
  shell's, built on `Dispatchers.Default` as Zápasy's list is) and `ReportsScreen`
  (nothing before the first answer; `NoReportScreen` for an empty device; otherwise a
  `LazyColumn` of two sections, a group with no report having no heading). `App.kt`
  has a `ReportsRoute`; the title is `reports_title`.
- **`ui/common/ReportStatusBadge.kt`, `ListHeading.kt`**: moved out of `FixturesScreen`,
  where they were private, and used by both lists.
- **Strings** (cs, en, uk): `reports_title`, `reports_working`, `reports_confirmed`.
- **Koin:** `ObserveReports`, `ReportsViewModel`.
- Comments brought level in `Navigation.kt`, `App.kt` and `NoReportScreen.kt`.

### What the JVM could not show

- **A real kill.** "After a kill" here is a new Koin graph in the same JVM over a
  database put in by hand. That the database survives the process is prompt 15 Gate 3's
  proof (a driver closed and another opened on the same file); that the app comes back to
  this list after one is the phone's.
- **The configuration change.** N2 proves the navigator's half: adopted twice, the referee
  stays on the list. That a rotation recomposes the shell and fires the adoption again is
  how Compose works on Android, not something seen here.
- **iOS.** Nothing was built for it; everything changed is common code.
- **A long list under a thumb**, a font at 130 %, the dark theme: the renders are 360 dp,
  100 %, light.

### Judgement calls

All four are in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, 2026-10-31 "0.4.0, Gate 1", each with a "Reverses
if".

1. **The list is beneath every report, however it was opened**, so Zpět on the header
   goes to the list even for a report opened from Zápasy.
2. **A match under way is adopted once per launch**, so a configuration change cannot pull
   a referee off the list they went back to.
3. **A row is a fixture's row in Zápasy without the pitch, the group always named**; the
   badge and the heading band are one composable each, shared by both lists.
4. **The words**: *Zápisy*, *Rozpracované*, *Potvrzené* (en *Reports*, *Being worked on*,
   *Confirmed*; uk *Протоколи*, *У роботі*, *Підтверджені*, not read by a native
   speaker); nothing at all before the first answer.

### Not done

- **Zápasy is unchanged**: an unsigned report still keeps its fixture under
  *Nadcházející* for good. Point 1's stray report is Gate 2's.
- **The en and uk empty state** still say *No report open* / *Протокол не відкрито*,
  written for a tab with nothing open in it. True on an empty device, but narrower than
  the Czech; not changed, as the brief says the screen says what it said.
- **No year** in a row's date, as in Zápasy.
- **The list does not mark the report open in the tab**, if one is: not asked for.
- `NavigationState.offersBack`'s `current.matchId != null` is redundant now (every step
  of a report has the list beneath it). Kept: harmless, and not this gate's.
- No emulator, no phone, no iOS.

### For the phone checklist (as first written; Gate 7 assembles it)

Install over 0.3.1 without uninstalling, with a report of each status on the phone.

## Gate 2 — a report can be discarded (point 1)

### What changed for a referee

- **Every row in the Zápis tab's list has a *Zahodit* of its own**: an outlined button
  under the group code, its word in red, a full touch target. Tapping the row still opens
  the report; only *Zahodit* discards, and there is no swipe or long press. Any report can
  be discarded, whatever its status: *Rozepsáno*, *Probíhá*, *Dohráno* and *Potvrzeno*.
- **It always asks first.** *Zahodit zápis?*, then the match (*Kominíci – Sp. Sumýš, 7. 9.
  v 18:00*), what goes (*Zahodí se: hlavička, obě sestavy, 3 góly, 1 karta, hodnocení.*,
  or *Zatím v něm nic není vyplněné.* for a stray tap) and *Zahození nejde vrátit zpět.*
  A signed report's question adds, in bold, that both captains and the referee signed it,
  that it may already have been sent to PSMF, and that files saved to the device's folder
  and mail already sent stay as they are.
- **The answers are *Ponechat zápis* and *Ano, zahodit*.** Keep is the filled button, in
  the place a tap out of habit goes (at 360 dp the two are stacked, keep on top). *Ano,
  zahodit* is plain red text, and **is greyed for the first second** after the question
  appears, so the second tap of a double tap on *Zahodit* cannot be the yes. Tapping
  beside the question keeps the report.
- **After the yes**: the report is gone from the list (*Žádný zápis* if it was the last)
  and from Zápasy: no badge on its fixture, and a fixture from an earlier day leaves
  *Nadcházející* again, as any fixture without a report does. If it was the match in
  progress, the tab's badge goes. The tab stays on the list. Tapping the fixture again
  starts a new, empty report, at the header.
- **What stays on the phone**: the card numbers and dates of birth the app remembers per
  player, jersey numbers corrected in Týmy, followed teams, and the files the export saved.

### What was done

- **`shared`, `usecase/DiscardReport.kt`** (new): `ReportContents` (what the question
  names: header, lineups, goals, cards, assessment, signatures; `signed`, `isEmpty`) and
  `DiscardReport`: `contentsOf(matchId)` reads it from the report and its drafts;
  `invoke(asked: ReportContents)` removes the report that question was about, through
  `MatchRepository.delete` (the report's rows, its lineup drafts and its pitch-added
  players, one transaction, as it already did). **It is the only caller of `delete`** in
  the app: `grep -rn '\.delete(' --include=*.kt` over every main source set finds it and
  `AndroidReportSender` clearing its own share-cache files (`java.io.File.delete`).
- **`ReportsViewModel`**: `ReportsEvent` (`DiscardRequested(row)`, `DiscardDismissed`,
  `DiscardConfirmed`); the question is state (`ReportsUiState.discarding`), asked with
  what `contentsOf` says; the yes takes the question on screen, clears it, then discards,
  so **a yes with no question up removes nothing**. `discarded`/`discardHandled()` tell
  the route, as Zápasy's `openMatch` does.
- **`ReportsScreen`**: *Zahodit* on every row; **`DiscardReportDialog`** (new): the
  question, keep in Material's confirm slot, the yes in the other, armed after `ARMING`
  (1 s).
- **`AppNavigator.discarded(matchId)`**: the report's steps leave the tab, and the shell
  never adopts it afterwards (judgement call 4). `App.kt`'s `ReportsRoute` calls it.
- **Strings** (cs, en, uk): `reports_discard*`, and the app's first `<plurals>` (goals,
  cards, signatures).
- **Koin**: `DiscardReport`; `ReportsViewModel` takes it.
- `AppShellTest`'s harness passes `onEvent = {}` to `ReportsScreen`, which now takes one.

### Judgement calls

All six are in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, 2026-10-31 "0.4.0, Gate 2", each with a "Reverses
if".

1. **The question counts what goes when it is asked**, from the report and its drafts;
   not the whistles, which the row's status says.
2. **The yes is never the one a quick tap lands on**: keep in the slot a habitual tap goes
   to (filled), and the yes disabled for the first second.
3. ***Zahodit* is an outlined button under the group code**, its word in the error colour,
   with narrower padding so the names keep their width at 360 dp.
4. **The navigator forgets a discarded report**: its steps leave the tab and it is never
   adopted after, against the shell's adoption arriving late.
5. **What stays on the device**: remembered card numbers and dates of birth, jersey
   overrides, followed teams, saved files, sent mail.
6. **The words**, and the app's first plurals; the Ukrainian not read by a native speaker.

### Not done

- **Discarding from inside the report** (the header, the recap): not asked for; the list is
  where it is.
- **A report from the retired 6-K placeholder** (before 0.2.0) cannot be discarded: Gate 1
  does not list it, having no fixture to draw. It stays in the database untouched.
- **Forgetting what was typed at a stray report** (a card number, a date of birth): the
  remembered tables do not record the match, so it would be schema 9.
- **No undo** of a discard, and no copy kept anywhere: the question says so.
- The *many* plural forms (Czech and Ukrainian decimals) are written but never used.
- No emulator, no phone, no iOS.

### For the phone checklist (as first written; Gate 7 assembles it)

Install over 0.3.1 without uninstalling, with a report of each status on the phone, and
one stray *Rozepsáno* report on a fixture from an earlier day.

1. **The stray report.** Zápasy first: the earlier day's fixture under *Nadcházející* with
   *Rozepsáno*. Zápis, *Zahodit* on its row. **Should:** *Zahodit zápis?*, the teams, date
   and time, *Zatím v něm nic není vyplněné.*, *Zahození nejde vrátit zpět.*; *Ano,
   zahodit* grey for a moment, then red. *Ano, zahodit*: the row gone; in Zápasy the
   fixture gone from *Nadcházející*. **Failure:** the report or the badge still there; the
   fixture still under *Nadcházející*.
2. **Keep, three ways.** On another report: *Zahodit*, then *Ponechat zápis*; *Zahodit*,
   then a tap beside the question; *Zahodit*, then Android's back. **Should:** each time the
   question goes and the report stays. **Failure:** any of them removes it.
3. **A double tap.** Double-tap *Zahodit* quickly, on rows at different heights in a list
   long enough to scroll. **Should:** the question, and the report still there.
   **Failure:** the report gone without a deliberate *Ano, zahodit*.
4. **What the question says.** *Zahodit* on a *Dohráno* report with goals and cards.
   **Should:** *Zahodí se:* with the header, the lineups, the right number of goals and
