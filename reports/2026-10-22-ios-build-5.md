### What dropping `materialIconsExtended` did

Sizes by `du -sm`, the unit the earlier reports used.

| `ComposeApp.framework` | Build 5 | Before |
|---|---|---|
| debug | **275 MB** (binary 271.0 MB) | 374 MB (2026-09-01) |
| release | **150 MB** (binary 155.2 MB) | 144 MB (2026-09-29), 147 MB (build 3) |

| Archived `iosApp.app` | Build 5 | Build 4 |
|---|---|---|
| bundle | **37,540 KB** | 37,328 KB |
| executable | **35,083,520 B** | 34,867,648 B |

- **Debug: −99 MB (−26%). Proved,** and it is at least that much, because the
  374 MB was measured on 2026-09-01 with much less of the app in it. There is
  no build-4 debug figure to compare like for like.
- **Release and the shipped app: no gain.** The archived app is 212 KB
  (0.6%) *larger* than build 4's. The release link was already dead-stripping
  the icons nothing used, so this is the expectation in `prompts/15` Gate 6
  confirmed. The growth is consistent with `prompts/15`'s new code (two
  tables, the shirt, the draft model), though nothing here separates the two.
