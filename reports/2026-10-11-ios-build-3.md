`baseConfigurationReference` on both target configurations, and **no
`DEVELOPMENT_TEAM`**.

**Verified the only way that counts:**

- `xcodebuild -showBuildSettings`: `DEVELOPMENT_TEAM` resolves in Debug and
  Release; with `Local.xcconfig` moved aside, it is simply unset and the build
  still configures. That is a fresh clone, or CI, building unsigned.
- **A signed build** (`-allowProvisioningUpdates`, Debug, generic iOS device):
  **succeeded in 239 s**. *Apple Development*, `iOS Team Provisioning
  Profile: *`, and the app's `TeamIdentifier` **equals the saved ID**, the
  same company team that signed build 1.
- **`git status` afterwards: empty.** Nothing under `iosApp/`, nothing
  anywhere.
- No committed file contains the ID; checked with a search of the working
  tree.

**The one habit this changes:** do not pick the team in Xcode's *Signing &
Capabilities* menu. The menu shows the team read from `Config.xcconfig`;
choosing it there writes `DEVELOPMENT_TEAM` back into `project.pbxproj`.
